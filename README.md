### 👨‍💻 About Me

I'm **Matías D'Agostino**, but everyone calls me **Tato**. I'm a Full-Stack developer from Paraná, Argentina, with a University Degree in Programming (**UTN**). I like building things people actually use: solid APIs, clean interfaces and apps that make someone's day easier.

- 🚀 **Building:** **Teire**, a free app to organize your day and share it with friends.
- 🛠️ **Daily stack:** **Java**, **Spring Boot**, **Python**, **FastAPI**, **TypeScript** and **React**.
- 🌱 **Learning:** **Tauri**, **Supabase** and data analysis with **R**.
- 💼 **Available** for collaborative projects and custom freelance development.
